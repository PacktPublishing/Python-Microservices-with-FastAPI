# Refund Policy

## Full Refunds
If a sitter cancels within 24 hours of the scheduled booking start time, parents receive a full refund including the platform service fee. The refund is processed automatically to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.

## Partial Refunds
For cancellations made between 24-48 hours before the booking start time, parents receive a 50% refund of the booking fee. The platform service fee is non-refundable in this case.

## No Refunds
Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the booking are non-refundable. However, parents can reschedule the booking without additional fees if the sitter agrees to the new time.

# Payment Processing

## When Payment Occurs
Payment is processed immediately when the booking is confirmed. The full amount including the platform service fee is charged to the parent's payment method on file.

## Sitter Compensation
The sitter receives 85% of the booking fee after the booking is completed. Payment is transferred to the sitter's linked bank account within 2-3 business days after the booking ends.

## Payment Methods
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, and PayPal. We do not accept cash, checks, cryptocurrency, or payment apps like Venmo or Zelle.

# Background Checks

## Required Verification
All sitters must complete a background check before accepting bookings. The check includes criminal records search, sex offender registry check, and identity verification.

## Background Check Process
Background checks are processed through our partner verification service. Results typically take 3-5 business days. Sitters cannot accept bookings until the check is complete and approved.

## Ongoing Monitoring
We perform continuous monitoring of all active sitters. If any concerning information appears after the initial check, the sitter's account may be suspended pending review.

# Trust and Safety

## Parent Safety Guidelines
Always meet sitters in a public place for the first meeting. Share your booking details with a trusted friend or family member. Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, cancel the booking.

## Sitter Safety Guidelines
Never share personal financial information with families. Report any inappropriate requests immediately. You have the right to leave any situation that feels unsafe.

## Emergency Procedures
In case of emergency, call 911 first. Then contact our 24/7 support line at 1-800-SITTERS. We will help coordinate with emergency services and notify the parents immediately.
